Smooth Outline — TEE to GREEN

Creates an estimated curved hole corridor using the imported hole route, or a straight line from TEE to GREEN if no route exists. In MOVE mode, drag the few blue centreline controls to follow a dogleg. Change the width to adjust the whole boundary smoothly.
